Sci-fi first-person puzzle game

Pythagoras

A sci-fi first-person puzzle game where you use trigonometry to stabilise triangle-based experiments aboard the Pythagoras space station. In active development, with a free playtest on itch.io.

When2025 - present
DurationSince August 2025, ongoing
My roleSolo developer: design, programming, level design and art assembly
TeamSolo project
UnityC#Low-poly 3DFirst-person

Pythagoras is a sci-fi first-person puzzle game set aboard the Pythagoras research station. As a new scientist on board, you use clues from your environment, and your knowledge of trigonometry, to build triangles out of everyday objects and operate the station’s advanced devices to keep its failing experiments running. It blends the challenge of a puzzle game with the exploration of an immersive sim, all wrapped in a retro-futuristic, low-poly aesthetic spread across four distinct biomes.

Challenge

How do you teach trigonometry so it feels like play rather than a lesson? Pythagoras turns the maths into tactile, first-person environmental puzzles: players manipulate objects to build triangles and operate the station’s devices to keep its experiments running.

What I built

Built solo in Unity with a low-poly, corporate retro-futuristic art style. I designed and scripted a set of hands-on trigonometry puzzles across four distinct biomes, from the sterile Trigonometric Core to the vibrant Geometric Garden, using multiple hand-crafted Retro Sci-Fi asset packs by Polynest from the Unity Asset Store for the station’s props and environments.

Result

In active development since August 2025, with a free public playtest build on itch.io and a Steam store page for a planned 2027 release.

What I learned

Planning a full game production from start to finish is a real challenge and thus fur thaught me important lessons in how to prototype but also how to get people to care that your project exists. So much about game development is not about technical and design capabilities but about management and organisation.
