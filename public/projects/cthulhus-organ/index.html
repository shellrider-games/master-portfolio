Cthulhu's organ

An underwater VR level built around a hand-played organ. Every key is mapped to a sampled church organ and mixed as an interactive, ambisonic soundscape, so the music, the sea creatures and Cthulhu all surround you.

UnityWwiseVRAmbisonicsInteractive Audio

Cthulhu’s organ is an underwater VR level created for a 360° Audio & Video course. It is built around sound: the instrument you play is a sampled church organ, and the entire mix is interactive and ambisonic, placing the organ, the sea creatures and Cthulhu around you in 3D space.

Challenge

A 360° Audio & Video course brief with one rule: sound had to be the main character. We had to design a VR experience where interactive audio, not the visuals, carries the story.

What we built

Cthulhu’s organ is an underwater VR level built around a playable organ. Played with hand tracking, each key triggers a sampled church organ, and the whole mix is built in Wwise as interactive, ambisonic audio that surrounds the player. As you play, sea creatures drift in to listen while Cthulhu, placed somewhere around you in the space, tries to steer you towards the notes that set him free.

My contribution

I owned the audio. I integrated Wwise into the VR application and built the interactive organ from samples of the Bureå church organ, mapping every key and shaping how it responds to the player. I also recorded and edited Cthulhu’s voicelines.

Result

A playable VR level and a 360° video capture of the experience.

What I learned

In this project I learned how to use Wwise to create interactive sounds.
