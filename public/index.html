About Me

I am an XR developer and researcher who also builds games. My strong suits are Unity and C#, and I enjoy the whole journey of a project, from prototyping and interactive systems to the DevOps and build pipelines that keep a team shipping. I am currently completing my Master in Interactive Technologies at USTP, and my game work ranges from commercial mobile and VR titles to my own first-person puzzle game. I also work with machine learning and statistics in Python, though my strengths lie in real-time XR and game development.
