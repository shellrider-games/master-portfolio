Pythagoras is a sci-fi first-person puzzle game set aboard the Pythagoras research station. As a new scientist on board, you use clues from your environment, and your knowledge of trigonometry, to build triangles out of everyday objects and operate the station's advanced devices to keep its failing experiments running. It blends the challenge of a puzzle game with the exploration of an immersive sim, all wrapped in a retro-futuristic, low-poly aesthetic spread across four distinct biomes.
